An online order is confirmed only when Layla Kitchen verifies the payment and the website reports that the purchase is confirmed. Returning from the payment provider does not by itself confirm payment or an order. A paid invoice confirms the order for the current one person operating workflow. Meals for the current Qatar calendar day cannot be ordered online. If a Daily Dish cart contains both current day and future selections, the current day selections are identified and excluded before the customer confirms the revised future order and amount. The customer may contact Layla Kitchen about current day availability, but contact does not guarantee acceptance.

An advance normal menu order uses one service date for the entire checkout. Each item shows its earliest available service date based on its preparation days, the current Qatar date and cutoff time, and closed service dates. The cart cannot be confirmed for a date earlier than the latest earliest date among its items. If availability, price, or eligibility changes before payment starts, the customer must review the updated server quote.

The server price shown in the final quote is authoritative. Current membership packages are QAR 900 for 20 meals and QAR 1,200 for 26 meals. Membership delivery is included. Delivery is also included in direct advance normal menu orders. No separate delivery fee or tax is added to those offerings under the current setup.

Promotion codes, membership meal allowances, and saved customer credit do not apply to advance normal menu orders. Those orders use their own cart and checkout, separate from Daily Dish and membership purchases.

Other prices may vary by menu, event size, customization, staffing, equipment needs, and other service requirements. Quotes may be valid for a limited period and may change when order details change.

## 5. Payments

Online payments are processed through SkipCash. Every purchase or renewal is initiated by the customer. Layla Kitchen does not automatically renew memberships or make recurring charges.

Payment is accepted only after confirmation from the payment provider. A completed online order creates a paid invoice. A paid membership records one payment for the package amount after any valid discount; the membership's daily meal invoices are allocated to that payment as meals are booked under the membership.

Layla Kitchen sends confirmation by email after a completed payment or membership purchase. An email delay or failure does not change the recorded payment or purchase result.

## 6. Memberships and meal selections

Paid memberships do not expire because of time. Unused meals remain available while the membership remains valid and subject to its recorded use, pause, and cancellation history.

A customer may purchase a membership without selecting every meal immediately and may return later to select the remaining meals without paying again. A customer may also purchase another membership. Membership allowances are used in purchase order, with the oldest remaining allowance used before the next one.

Membership meal credits are not tied to calendar days. A customer may select one or several eligible future main dishes for the same date or across different dates, up to the remaining meal allowance. Each selected main dish quantity counts as one meal. Included sides do not use additional meal credits.

Unless a confirmed booking shows a different deadline, a future membership meal selection may be changed or cancelled online before 11:00 PM Qatar time on the calendar day before service. The deadline recorded when the booking is confirmed applies to that booking. An eligible cancellation restores the corresponding meal quantity to the original membership allowance. If an approved pause covers future booked meals, those bookings are cancelled and their eligible meal quantities are restored.

## 7. Promotion codes

Membership promotion codes are created and shared by Layla Kitchen. A code may be limited by eligible plan, start and end date, total uses, uses per customer, and whether it applies to a first membership purchase, a renewal, or both. Only one promotion code may be used for one membership purchase. Promotion codes do not apply to Daily Dish pay per dish orders or advance normal menu orders and do not create customer credit.

A partial discount reduces the membership price only. It does not reduce the number of meals or change how meal credits are used.

When a valid promotion reduces a membership price to zero, the website creates only a pending meal plan request for staff review. It does not create a payment, paid invoice, active membership, meal allowance, or confirmed meal booking at that time. A promotion that makes the price zero may be redeemed only once per customer. Submitting the valid request uses that redemption, and later rejection or cancellation does not restore it.

Payments are not refunded through this website flow. When a paid amount is released or retained after an authorized cancellation or correction, it remains recorded as customer credit. Customer credit does not expire, but customers cannot apply it themselves on the website. Only an authorized Layla Kitchen administrator may allocate customer credit in the RMS.

Editing an invoice does not cancel an order. When Layla Kitchen voids the invoice linked to an order, that order is treated as cancelled. For an advance normal menu order, the payment allocation is released and the paid amount remains as unallocated customer credit for later administrator allocation. No refund is created. For an eligible membership meal, the payment allocation is released and the meal quantity is restored to its original membership allowance.
