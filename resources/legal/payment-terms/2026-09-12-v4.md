## 3. Orders and confirmation

An online order is confirmed only when Layla Kitchen verifies payment and the website reports that the purchase is confirmed. Returning from the payment provider does not by itself confirm payment or an order. Under the current operating workflow, a paid invoice confirms the order but does not represent a separate delivered status.

Meals for the current Qatar calendar day cannot be ordered online. Current-day selections are excluded before the customer confirms any remaining future order and amount. Contacting Layla Kitchen about current-day availability does not guarantee acceptance.

An advance normal menu order uses one service date for the entire checkout. Its service date must satisfy every selected item's preparation period, the Qatar cutoff, and closed dates. Availability, price, and eligibility are confirmed again before payment.

## 4. Prices, delivery, and tax

The server price in the final quote is authoritative. Current membership packages are QAR 900 for 20 meals and QAR 1,200 for 26 meals. Delivery is included for memberships and direct advance normal menu orders. No separate delivery fee or tax is added under the current setup.

Optional checkout add-ons use the current RMS menu price for the selected service date. They are added to the same dated order, invoice, and SkipCash payment as the related Daily Dish, membership purchase, covered membership booking, or advance normal menu checkout.

Promotion codes, membership meal allowances, and saved customer credit do not apply to advance normal menu orders. Other prices may vary by menu, customization, staffing, equipment, and service requirements.

## 5. Payments

Online payments are processed through SkipCash. Every purchase or renewal is initiated by the customer. Layla Kitchen does not automatically renew memberships or make recurring charges.

Payment is accepted only after provider confirmation. A completed online order creates a paid invoice. A paid membership records one payment for the package amount after any valid membership discount, plus any separately priced checkout add-ons. Dated meal invoices are allocated to that payment as meals are booked.

Email confirmation may be delayed or fail without changing the recorded payment or purchase result.

## 6. Memberships and meal selections

Paid memberships do not expire because of time. A customer may purchase without selecting every meal, return later to select the balance without paying again, and purchase additional memberships. Allowances are consumed in purchase order, oldest first.

Meal credits are not tied to calendar days. Each selected main-dish quantity consumes one meal; included sides do not consume additional credits. Optional checkout add-ons never consume meal credits. A covered membership booking without add-ons requires no new payment. If add-ons are selected, only their current price is paid through SkipCash.

Unless the booking records another deadline, a future membership selection may be changed or cancelled online before 11:00 PM Qatar time on the day before service. Eligible cancellation or pause restores the meal quantity to its original membership allowance.

## 7. Promotion codes

Membership promotion codes are created and shared by Layla Kitchen. A code may be limited by plan, dates, total uses, per-customer uses, and first-purchase or renewal eligibility. Only one code may apply to a membership purchase. Promotion codes do not create customer credit.

A membership discount changes only the membership package price. It never reduces meal count and never discounts optional checkout add-ons.

When a valid promotion reduces the membership price to zero, the website creates only a pending meal plan request. It creates no payment, paid invoice, active membership, meal allowance, confirmed booking, or add-on order. A 100% promotion is redeemable once per customer, and later rejection or cancellation does not restore it.

## 8. Changes, cancellations, refunds, and customer credit

Payments are not refunded through this website flow. Released paid amounts remain customer credit and may be allocated only by an authorized RMS administrator.

Editing an invoice does not cancel an order. Voiding the invoice linked to an order treats the order as cancelled and releases its payment allocation. For an eligible membership meal, the meal quantity returns to its original allowance. The original payment remains recorded and no refund is created. These rules also apply when the invoice includes optional checkout add-ons.
